Nissan Key Information

Why Nissan owners often need a specialist

Nissan's NATS (Nissan Anti-Theft System) immobiliser requires any new key to be registered to the vehicle using dedicated diagnostic equipment before the engine will start. This applies to both standard transponder keys and the Intelligent Key proximity system. All-keys-lost situations may require additional pin-code extraction steps.

Nissan owners may need a specialist rather than a general key cutter when a key is lost, a key is damaged, or a key stops responding.
