At Your Location

Ignition diagnosis and repair — at your location

Ignition faults can range from a worn barrel that no longer turns smoothly, to a stuck key, a sheared wafer, or an electronic fault preventing the immobiliser from releasing. Our technician diagnoses the specific cause before recommending a course of action. In many cases, a worn barrel can be repaired or replaced on-site without requiring the vehicle to be transported to a garage or dealership.

Where a barrel replacement is required, the new barrel typically needs to be coded or programmed to accept the existing key so you do not need a new key cut at the same time. Our technician carries the equipment to complete this coding step on-site for supported vehicles.

Car Key Types We Work With

Different vehicles, different key systems

Not sure which key your vehicle uses? Give us your vehicle make, model and registration and our team can advise you on the available options.

Standard Car Keys

Some older vehicles use straightforward mechanical keys. These may only require accurate cutting to match the vehicle lock.

Transponder Keys

Many vehicles use a transponder chip as part of their immobiliser system. The electronic chip may need programming to the vehicle, not just key cutting.

Remote Key Fobs

Remote keys combine a physical key with electronic buttons. Depending on the vehicle, replacement may involve both key cutting and electronic programming.

Smart & Keyless Keys

Modern vehicles can use proximity or smart-key systems allowing keyless entry and push-button starting. These require vehicle-specific programming equipment.
