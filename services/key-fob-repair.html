Is Your Fob Repairable?

Not every fob problem needs a full replacement

A lot of common fob issues come down to a flat internal battery, worn buttons, a cracked case or a connection that's come loose � all of which can often be repaired without needing a brand-new key. Our technician can inspect the fob at your location and give you an honest assessment before recommending a repair or a replacement.

Where the fob itself is fine but the vehicle has simply stopped recognising it, reprogramming alone may resolve the issue.

Common Key Fob Problems

What we can help with

Unresponsive Buttons

Buttons that feel spongy, stick, or don't trigger the lock/unlock function reliably.

Flat or Failing Battery

A short remote range or an intermittent connection is often just a battery needing replacement.

Fob Not Recognised

Sometimes the fob works but the vehicle has stopped recognising it � often fixed with reprogramming.

Cracked or Damaged Casing

A worn, split or damaged outer casing can expose the internal electronics to moisture and dirt.

Lost or Stolen Fob

A missing fob may need deactivating from the vehicle's system for security, alongside a replacement.

Smart Key Not Starting the Car

Proximity and push-button start systems can develop faults that stop the vehicle recognising the key.
