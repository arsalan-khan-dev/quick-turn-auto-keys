Who We Are

Your mobile automotive locksmith team

Quick Turn Auto Keys is focused on providing convenient automotive locksmith services directly to customers. Instead of making you arrange vehicle recovery or find a workshop when your vehicle cannot be driven, our mobile approach allows us to come to your location for suitable services.

We work with a wide range of vehicle makes and key systems and use professional equipment to handle supported key cutting, programming, diagnostics and locksmith requirements.
